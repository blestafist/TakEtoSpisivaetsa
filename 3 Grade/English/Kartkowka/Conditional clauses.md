**Conditional clauses – alternatives to _if_**

- You use **unless** to mean 'if not' with the sense 'except if'.
    
    _I wouldn't lie to you unless it was necessary._
    
    (= _... if it wasn't necessary._)
    
- You use **imagine** or **suppose/supposing** to ask questions.
    
    _Imagine you were getting married_, how would you feel?
    
    (= _If you were getting married, ..._)
    
- You use **provided/providing** to create a condition.
    
    _I'll lend you €10 provided you pay me back tomorrow._
    
    (= _if you pay me back ..._)
    
- You can use **inversion** in formal contexts – mostly with the auxiliary verbs _were_, _had_ or _should_.
    
    _Had they followed_ my instructions, they wouldn't have got lost. (= _If they had followed ..._)
    

**Note:** In inversion, you do not contract negative forms.

_Had they not called_, we wouldn't have known (...).

NOT ~~_Hadn't they called ..._~~
