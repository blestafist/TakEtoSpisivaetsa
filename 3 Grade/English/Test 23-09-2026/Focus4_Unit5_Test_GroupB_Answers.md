# Focus 4 Second Edition (B2/B2+) — Unit 5 Test — Group B — Answers

This covers the Pearson Unit 5 test in both of the versions found online (same questions, different exercise numbers):

- **Polish version:** "Test Unit 5" / "MiniMatura Unit 5", © Pearson Central Europe 2020, 50 points. The *1kol* and *2kol* files are just one-column and two-column layouts of the same test. This is the main answer list below.
- **International version:** "Unit Test 5", © Pearson 2020, in two parts (Vocabulary, Grammar & Use of English; Dictation, Listening & Reading). See the section at the end; two exercises differ.

There is **no Group C** for this test.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Statement | **B** | **F** | **C** | **A** | **D** |

Extra statement: **E**.

## 2. Collocations — 5 pts
Extra word in list A: *apply*

1. I only found my **one true calling**…
2. There was a **sharp increase**…
3. I'm jealous of **part-timers**.
4. …follow that particular **career path**?
5. People who **have a vocation** are lucky.

## 3. Missing words (first letters given) — 5 pts
1. You never **give** up.
2. She really found her **niche**…
3. …many people lack **purpose** in their jobs…
4. …usually remains **constant**.
5. There's been a **rapid** decline…

## 4. Reported speech — 5 pts
1. Joe wanted to know **why I was still working** there.
2. He blamed **them for all his** failures.
3. He told me **not to worry about his raise** too much. *(also accepted: that I shouldn't worry about his raise)*
4. Tim explained **that they were looking into various** career prospects.
5. Jonny suggested **asking the teacher** for help. *(also accepted: (that) I ask the teacher)*

## 5. Words in brackets (max 6 words) — 5 pts
1. The woman **claimed to be our new boss**
2. The CEO **insisted on closing the whole factory** down
3. My parents **warned me not to quit** my job
4. She **suggested signing the contract** as soon as possible
5. His teacher **urged him to study** hard

## 6. Use of English — key word transformations — 5 pts
1. …hard to **put up with** my younger brother. (PUT)
2. We've **run out of** coffee. (OUT)
3. I'm going to be **tied up with** this work… (UP)
4. …famous people you **look up to**? (TO)
5. I've **come up with** a great idea. (COME)

## 7. Translation (max 6 words) — 5 pts
1. I'll **get round to tidying** my bedroom… *(also accepted: get down to tidying)*
2. The man **denied lying** about his qualifications. *(also accepted: denied that he had lied)*
3. A wise woman once **advised me to listen** to my own heart.
4. They finally managed to **persuade him to stay**… *(also accepted: convince him to stay)*
5. The shop keeper **accused me of stealing** a chocolate bar.

## How these answers were checked
- **Official keys:** the Pearson Poland key and both international Unit Test 5 keys agree on every item.
- **Questions:** every answer was checked against the actual test papers (both versions), including grammar, fit in the gap, word limits and letter counts. For example, international Ex 6.1 must be "round" (5 letters), not "down" (4).
- **Blind re-solve:** an independent agent solved the whole test without the key. Its answers matched, apart from the p____ gap in Ex 3.3, where it guessed a different word. "Purpose" is the key's answer and it is Unit 5 vocabulary ("lack purpose").
- **Listening:** checked against the official Pearson *Focus 4 2E Tests Audioscript* (Unit 5). Each speaker matches only one statement.
