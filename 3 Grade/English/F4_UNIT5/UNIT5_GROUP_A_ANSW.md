## 2. Collocations — 5 pts

## 4. Reported speech — 5 pts
1. My boss warned **me not to take another** day off.
2. Dave suggested **asking Meg** about it. *(also accepted: that I (should) ask Meg)*
3. Janet refused **to accept a poorly-paid** job.
4. Mark said **(that) Sue hadn't applied for** any other jobs.
5. He asked **if/whether I would be staying with them** one more night. *(also accepted: we would be staying)*

## 5. Words in brackets (max 6 words) — 5 pts
1. she **recommended looking for a new job**
2. the owners of the factory **persuaded the workers to sign** the new contracts
3. The boss **begged her not to quit** her job
4. Everyone **accused him of stealing**
5. The man **thanked us for completing** the project

## 6. Use of English — key word transformations — 5 pts
1. We **came up against** many problems… (UP)
2. …which **made up for** the one she broke. (FOR)
3. I **stood up for** my brother… (STOOD)
4. Have you **got round to checking** my homework? (ROUND)
5. …people who **look down on** me. (DOWN)

## 7. Translation (max 6 words) — 5 pts
1. My parents **urged me to finish** school…
2. She **explained (that) she had come back** only the day before. *(also accepted: had returned)*
3. The boy **didn't admit (to) doing** anything wrong. *(also accepted: didn't admit that he had done)*
4. The prices of houses will **remain constant** for the next year or two.
5. I can't believe I **came up with such a** great idea…
