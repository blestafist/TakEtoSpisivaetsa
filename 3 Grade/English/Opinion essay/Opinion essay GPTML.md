An opinion essay

Introduction
	• Introduce the topic and state your main opinion.
	• Say which two (or more) points of view or aspects will be discussed.
	• To explain the $^1$reasons for my view, I will examine the issue in $^2\text{term}$ of ...
	• It is my intention to explore this issue from the viewpoint of ...
	• This essay will look at this question from the perspective of ...

Paragraphs 2 and 3
	• Discuss different aspects of the issue and support the main opinion you stated in the introduction.
	• When it $^3\text{comes}$ to ...
	• It is also important to consider this issue with $^4\text{regard}$ ? to ...
	• Another important angle on/aspect of this issue is ...
	• Expand the discussion by giving examples.

Paragraph 4
	• Acknowledge an opposing opinion and say why you disagree with it.
	• It is $^5\text{true}$ that ...
	• However, I am of the $^6\text{opinion}$ ? that ...
	• While some people say ..., I ...
	• Some people do not agree and feel that ...

Conclusion
	• Use a linking phrase to introduce a conclusion.
	In conclusion,/To sum up,/All things considered,/$^7\text{All in all}$,
	• Restate your main opinion.
	• Include a final comment.
