Look at sth for a **long** time:
- with interest, wonder or delight → gaze
- carefully and with difficulty → peer → вглядываться
- in surprise, anger or boredom → stare → пялится

Look at sth for a short time:
- and then look away → glance → бросить / скользить взлядом
- too quickly to see it clearly → glimpse → мельком
- secretly (through a keyhole) → peep → подглядывать

## Relative clauses → a part of sentence you can leave out without changing the sense of sentence
- Defining → you can leave out *who, that, where*. Without a comma
- Non-defining → separated by comma and *who, that, where* cannot be left out
- Refuced → for active: ing → for passive → 3rd form 
- Comment → refer to a whole sentence, using ***which***
