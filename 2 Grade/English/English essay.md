Conflicts in families is nothing unusual. Most of them often happen when parent's child is becoming a teenager. Because that's the time when a child experiences a mix of different emotions, and can't decide which of them he should accept or decline. Some of them begin misbehaving, others become argumentative, or provocative to their parents. With all of that said, we as grown people should understand them. We mustn't punish them for a natural process of being an adolescent. I know that some of you might be short-tempered, and you can't stand that kind of behavior, so in this particular essay I am going to go through "most common reasons of conflicts between a parent and his child".  

One of the most common sources of conflict is the growing need inside of every child for autonomy. Teenagers often want to make their own life decisions and choices, but parents still have to have an influence on their life, regardless of how responsible the child is. And here is where the problem comes from. A parent's need collides with their child's desire, this often leads to frustration on both sides. So if you want to resolve it, or completely avoid it, I recommend you once in a while taking your child out for a walk on a beautiful sunny day, maybe buy him ice-cream, sit and talk. Let him express his needs. Now, be very careful, because that's the place where a lot of people lose their temper. I want you to put yourself in his position, look at the world from his perspective and try to understand where it all comes from. Once you've done that, talk through with him about the conflicts you had before, and try to find a good solution for both sides.

The second pretty common reason for conflicts, can be lack of communication between a parent and his teenager. A human is a social nature, it can not exist on its own, that is why its crucial for parents to talk with their children about anything that is going on in their teenager's life. Lack of communication often leads to a feeling of being unheard, misunderstood. Some adolescent can't express their feeling or emotion properly, and in some cases it can lead to emotional problems such as anxiety and depression. To make sure that this never happens in you family, I would suggest to strengthen communication, I know that it might seems as a peace of cake, but in reality it can take up a lot of time. Strengthening communication can only be done by listening, understanding other side, creating a save space for honesty and thought where all of them could coexist. This can significantly reduce and help building a healthier and more trusting relationship with you young adults.
