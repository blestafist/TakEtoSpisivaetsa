## 📝 Reported Speech: Tense Shift Rules

| Исходное время (Direct Speech) | В какое переходит (Reported Speech)       |
| :----------------------------- | :---------------------------------------- |
| **Present Simple**             | Past Simple                               |
| **Present Continuous**         | Past Continuous                           |
| **Present Perfect**            | Past Perfect                              |
| **Present Perfect Continuous** | Past Perfect Continuous                   |
| **Past Simple**                | Past Perfect                              |
| **Past Continuous**            | Past Perfect Continuous                   |
| **Past Perfect**               | Past Perfect *(без изменений)*            |
| **Past Perfect Continuous**    | Past Perfect Continuous *(без изменений)* |
| **Future Simple**              | `would`                                   |
| **Future Continuous**          | `would be` + V-ing                        |
| **"is going to"**              | `was going to`                            |
