## Why Do Parents Never Understand Us?

Have you ever had an argument with your parents? I think every teenager measured with that problem. Today I want to tell you about the most common problems and the easiest ways to solve them without any pain.

---

In my opinion, the most common reason of having arguments with parents is difference between understanding freedom. Parents often don't understand that their children had already grown up and need more space. For example, my parents always ask me where I am, who I'm meeting with and what time I will come back home.

The second big problem is phone and internet. Most parents think that young people spend too much time online. My mom always says "put that phone away!" but all my friends are sitting online and not going to meet me in person :( 

---

But how can we solve that conflicts? There are several ways, but in my opinion, the best way is just to sit down and talk calmly, without shouting. Both sides should listen to an "opponent" and try to understand their point of view. Second way, but not pretty effecient is compromise. For example, if you want to come home later, you should always call and say where you are. It's fair for both sides.

---

Is it possible for parents and teenagers to live without conflicts? I believe yes, but only when both sides respect each other. Maybe we should think more about this - how do you solve conflicts in your family?
